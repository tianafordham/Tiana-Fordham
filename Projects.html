My Projects
In Rotation
Event Marketing


In May, I organised an indie gig featuring three emerging artists. The poster design reflected the location (The Globe), and the name referred to both the Earth rotating and the rotation of artists throughout the night. I chose cool colours to suggest an evening event, muted tones which are less corporate, and pastels to appear youthful and inviting.
