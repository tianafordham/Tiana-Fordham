Hello!
I'm Tiana, a second-year BA Music Management student. I am fascinated by the way people experience and interact with the world around them. Whether through music, theatre, or art, I love seeing the impact creativity can have on people.

I'm particularly drawn to the collaborative side of it. Bringing together people with different perspectives and skills creates opportunities to learn from one another, experience the world in a different way, and create something that can have a lasting impact on others.

As passionate as I am about the concepts and ideas behind creative projects, I also enjoy the process of organising and facilitating them. To me, creativity is as much about inspiring others as it is about expressing yourself.
